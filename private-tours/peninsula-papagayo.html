Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Wild Papagayo tours private?

Yes. Every tour is private for your group, with your own vehicle, guide and pace, not a shared bus with other travelers.

Do you pick up directly from Peninsula Papagayo resorts?

Yes. We offer direct pickup from Four Seasons, Andaz, Nekajui and other resorts across Peninsula Papagayo and the wider Guanacaste area.

What are the best tours from Peninsula Papagayo?

Popular options include the Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience, Rio Celeste, Rincon de la Vieja and the Buena Vista Mega Combo, depending on whether you want wildlife, scenery or adventure.

What is the best wildlife tour from Papagayo?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience and the Palo Verde River Boat Tour are our top wildlife-focused options, each with a different pace and terrain.

Can we customize our tour?

Yes. Because every tour is private, pickup time, pacing and add-ons can be tailored to your group. Tell us your dates and interests and we'll help build the day.

Are private tours suitable for families?

Yes. Many of our tours are family-friendly and paced accordingly. Tell us the age of your children and we can point you to the tours that fit best.

How far are Rio Celeste and Rincon de la Vieja from Papagayo?

Rio Celeste is roughly 2 to 2.5 hours by private transfer, and Rincon de la Vieja's Las Pailas sector is roughly 1.7 to 2.2 hours, based on real driving distance.

Can you help us choose a tour based on how much time we have?

Yes. Tell us how many days you have and your travel style, and your Wild Papagayo concierge can recommend the best mix of closer and farther destinations.
