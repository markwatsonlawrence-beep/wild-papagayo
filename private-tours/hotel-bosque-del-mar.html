Beyond the Beach

Explore Beyond Hotel Bosque del Mar

Hotel Bosque del Mar is a boutique beachfront hotel on Playa Hermosa, Guanacaste, approximately 26 to 33 minutes from Liberia airport (LIR). Its beach-town setting, tropical gardens and walkable restaurants make it one of the most relaxed bases in the region for private day trips into northern Costa Rica.

Wild Papagayo organizes private tours with direct pickup from Hotel Bosque del Mar. Every experience departs from and returns to the hotel, with no shared buses, no other travel groups and no fixed schedule to follow.

Day trips range from about six hours to full days of eight to twelve hours in Costa Rica's national parks, volcanic landscapes, cloud forest and wildlife reserves. Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking based on your selected experience and travel date.

How Far Are Costa Rica's Top Experiences from Playa Hermosa?

Playa Hermosa is a practical base for private day trips into Guanacaste's national parks and natural landmarks. All destinations served from Hotel Bosque del Mar are reachable in a single day, though exact travel time depends on the specific pickup location, destination and road conditions on the day of your tour.

Shorter routes — Rincón de la Vieja, Miravalles, Palo Verde — allow morning departures with ample time at the destination. Longer routes — Río Celeste and Monteverde — are full-day commitments with earlier starts. Your Wild Papagayo guide will confirm the recommended pickup time from Hotel Bosque del Mar when your booking is finalized.

For Couples

Private Tours for Couples at Hotel Bosque del Mar

Hotel Bosque del Mar is a boutique beachfront hotel suited to couples who want a quieter beach-town base. Every Wild Papagayo tour is private — no other travelers on your schedule, with the experience adapting to your pace.

Nature & Scenery

Miravalles Volcano Waterfalls & Spa and Celeste River Hike offer full days in natural settings that feel nothing like a resort day — volcanic mud, blue water and tropical forest at close range.

Wildlife & Adventure

Hanging Bridges & Sloths and Rincón de la Vieja Hiking are ideal for couples who want a mix of wildlife encounters, forest walks and volcanic landscapes.

Relaxed Pace

Waterfall & Las Pumas is the shorter option — about six hours — for couples who want to explore Guanacaste without filling the full day.

For Families

Private Tours for Families Staying at Hotel Bosque del Mar

Several Wild Papagayo experiences are well suited to families with children. All tours are private, so your group travels at its own pace with a dedicated bilingual guide.

Wildlife Encounters

Hanging Bridges & Sloths and Waterfall & Las Pumas are family favorites — a mix of suspension bridges, waterfalls and rescued wildlife at Las Pumas center, including pumas, jaguars and monkeys.

Active Days

Rincón de la Vieja Hiking and Celeste River Hike are suitable for older children and families comfortable with full-day hikes through national parks. Minimum age 8 for Celeste River.

Learning Experiences

Monteverde Hanging Bridges & Coffee Tour combines cloud forest wildlife with a guided coffee and chocolate plantation tour — an engaging day for families curious about Costa Rica's food culture.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Wild Papagayo arrange pickup directly from Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Yes. Wild Papagayo coordinates direct hotel pickup from Hotel Bosque del Mar in Playa Hermosa. Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking based on your selected tour and travel date.

What private tours are available from Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Six tours are featured in this guide: Hanging Bridges & Sloths, Miravalles Volcano Waterfalls & Spa, Rincón de la Vieja Hiking, Celeste River Hike, Monteverde Hanging Bridges & Coffee Tour, and Waterfall & Las Pumas. All include direct hotel pickup.

How far is Hotel Bosque del Mar from Liberia Airport?

About 26 to 33 minutes by private transfer from Guanacaste Airport (LIR), roughly 24.4 km away.

Do I need a rental car if I'm staying at Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Not necessarily. Guests who use organized private tours and airport transfers can typically manage the trip without renting a car.

How long is the drive from Hotel Bosque del Mar to Rincón de la Vieja?

Travel time from Playa Hermosa depends on the specific pickup location and road conditions on the day. Your Wild Papagayo guide will confirm the recommended departure time when your booking is finalized. The Rincón de la Vieja Hiking tour itself takes about 8 hours and runs Tuesday through Sunday — the national park is closed on Mondays.

Are tours from Hotel Bosque del Mar private?

Yes. Every Wild Papagayo experience is reserved exclusively for your group. No shared buses, no other travelers added to your group.

What is the best private tour from Hotel Bosque del Mar for couples?

Miravalles Volcano Waterfalls & Spa and Celeste River Hike are both popular with couples for their full-day natural settings. Hanging Bridges & Sloths is a strong option for wildlife and suspension bridges. For a shorter day, Waterfall & Las Pumas takes about 6 hours.
