Beyond the Resort

Explore Costa Rica Beyond Peninsula Papagayo

Four Seasons Resort Costa Rica puts you in one of Guanacaste's most extraordinary coastal settings. When you're ready to explore beyond the resort, Wild Papagayo connects your stay with private journeys into Costa Rica's volcanoes, tropical forests, wildlife areas, waterfalls and rural communities.

Every experience is private to your group, with pickup directly from Four Seasons, dedicated transportation and a local guide. Rather than adapting your day to a shared excursion schedule, we help shape the experience around you.

Book Direct. Explore Privately. Work directly with a local Costa Rican operator specializing in private experiences from Papagayo.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best private tours from Four Seasons Costa Rica?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience, Rio Celeste, Rincon de la Vieja and the Buena Vista Mega Combo are among our most popular picks for Four Seasons guests, depending on whether you want wildlife, scenery or adventure.

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly from Four Seasons Resort Costa Rica?

Yes. Every tour includes private pickup directly from Four Seasons, coordinated around your selected experience and departure time.

Are the tours completely private?

Yes. Every tour is private for your group, with your own vehicle, guide and pace, not a shared bus with other travelers.

What is the best wildlife tour from Four Seasons?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience and the Palo Verde River Boat Tour are our top wildlife-focused options, each with a different pace and terrain.

Can we visit Rio Celeste from Four Seasons Costa Rica?

Yes. Rio Celeste is a full-day private tour from Four Seasons, best booked in the December–April dry season for the most vivid water color.

What is the best tour for families staying at Four Seasons?

It depends on the age of your children: Palo Verde and wildlife tours suit younger kids, while hanging bridges and adventure parks give older children and teens more to do.

Can Wild Papagayo customize our itinerary?

Yes. Because every tour is private, pickup time, pacing and add-ons can be tailored to your group. Tell us your dates and interests and we'll help build the day.

How far are the main attractions from Four Seasons Costa Rica?

Most destinations run roughly 1.5 to 2.5 hours by private transfer, with Arenal being the longest at around 3 to 3.5 hours. See the travel-time table above for specific destinations.
