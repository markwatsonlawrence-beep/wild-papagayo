Beyond the Resort

Explore Beyond Hotel Riu Guanacaste

Hotel Riu Guanacaste is a large all-inclusive resort on Playa Matapalo, approximately 42 to 54 minutes from Liberia airport (LIR). The resort's water park, pools and beach keep most guests entertained on-site — but Guanacaste's most memorable experiences are a short private drive away.

Every tour in this guide departs from and returns to Hotel Riu Guanacaste. Day trips range from a shorter half-day outing of about six hours to full days of eight or nine hours in Costa Rica's national parks, volcanic landscapes and wildlife reserves. Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking based on your selected experience and travel date.

One group. One vehicle. One guide. No shared buses. No other travelers on your schedule.

For Families

Private Tours for Families at Hotel Riu Guanacaste

Hotel Riu Guanacaste is designed with families in mind — Splash Water World, a children's pool and RIULand kids' programming all keep younger travelers happy on-site. When the family is ready for an off-site day, every Wild Papagayo tour is private, so the pace stays your own.

Easy Half-Day Wildlife

Palo Verde River Boat Tour is an Easy, covered boat tour suitable from age 2. Perfect for families with young children who want wildlife without a demanding pace — typically back by early afternoon.

Wildlife Full Day

Hanging Bridges & Sloths is rated Easy and suits every age. Three suspension bridges, a waterfall swim stop and a guided sloth encounter — consistently the tour families thank us for most.

Active Families

Congo Trail's half-day adventure (zip lines, ATV, horseback) works well for families with older children. Minimum age is flexible — ask us and we'll confirm suitability for your group.

For Couples

Private Tours for Couples at Hotel Riu Guanacaste

Every Wild Papagayo tour is private — no shared vans, no other travelers. Couples at Riu Guanacaste often use one free day for a full nature or adventure experience, then return to the all-inclusive by evening.

Nature & Scenery

Celeste River Hike offers a full day at one of Costa Rica's most photographed natural wonders. Moderate effort, long drive, high reward — best suited for the dry season (December–April) when the trail is dry and the river color is at its best.

Adventure Combo

Buena Vista Mega Combo packs zip lining, horseback riding, a mountain waterslide and volcanic hot springs into one private full day. Active, varied and a good use of one day if you want to do a lot.

Shorter Day

Congo Trail's half-day adventure leaves the afternoon free for the pools and resort. Good option when you want an experience without spending the whole day away.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Wild Papagayo arrange pickup directly from Hotel Riu Guanacaste?

Yes. Wild Papagayo coordinates direct hotel pickup from Hotel Riu Guanacaste at Playa Matapalo. Exact pickup time is confirmed after booking based on your selected tour and travel date.

What private tours are available from Hotel Riu Guanacaste?

Five tours are featured in this guide: Hanging Bridges & Sloths, Congo Trail Half-Day Adventure, Buena Vista Mega Combo, Palo Verde River Boat Tour and Celeste River Hike. All include direct hotel pickup and a private vehicle and guide for your group.

Which tour is best for families with young children at Riu Guanacaste?

The Palo Verde River Boat Tour is rated Easy, starts from age 2 and runs about 6 hours — a good choice for families with younger children. Hanging Bridges & Sloths is also rated Easy, includes lunch and suits every age group.

How long is the drive from Riu Guanacaste to Río Celeste?

As general Papagayo-area planning context, the drive is approximately 110 to 140 minutes each way. Actual travel time depends on road conditions and your exact pickup point. The hike itself is about 9 hours total. Passport details for all guests are required for the national park entry.

Are the tours private — not shared with other guests?

Yes. Every Wild Papagayo experience is reserved exclusively for your group. No shared vans, no other travelers added to your tour.

What is the difference between the Congo Trail and the Buena Vista Mega Combo?

Congo Trail is a half-day adventure (about 6 hours) combining zip lines, ATV and horseback riding — a good option when you want to be back at the resort by early afternoon. Buena Vista Mega Combo is a full day (about 8 hours) with zip lining, horseback riding, a mountain waterslide, hot springs and volcanic mud. Minimum age for Buena Vista is 8.

How far is Hotel Riu Guanacaste from Liberia Airport?

Hotel Riu Guanacaste on Playa Matapalo is approximately 42 to 54 minutes from Daniel Oduber Quiros International Airport (LIR), based on verified driving distance data.
