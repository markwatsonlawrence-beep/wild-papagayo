Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly from Nekajui?

Yes. Every private journey includes pickup directly from Nekajui, coordinated around your selected experience and departure time.

Are all experiences completely private?

Yes. Every journey is private for your group, with your own vehicle, guide and pace, never combined with other travelers.

Can you customize a private journey from Nekajui?

Yes. Tell your concierge what you'd like to experience, and we'll help shape a private itinerary around your available time.

What is the best wildlife experience from Nekajui?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience and the Palo Verde River Boat Tour are our top wildlife-focused journeys, each with a different pace.

Can we combine multiple experiences in one day?

Some full-day journeys, such as Costa Rica Highlights, already combine several destinations. For a fully custom combination, tell your concierge and we'll help plan it.

How far is Rio Celeste from Nekajui?

Roughly 114 to 146 minutes by private transfer, based on real driving distance from Nekajui.

Can you recommend an experience based on how much time we have?

Yes. Tell us how much time you'd like to spend beyond the resort and your Wild Papagayo concierge can recommend the journey that fits best.

Can Wild Papagayo arrange both airport transportation and tours?

Yes. The same local team can coordinate your private airport transfer and your private journeys during your stay.
