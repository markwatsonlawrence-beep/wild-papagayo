Why It Matters

Costa Rica Is Beautiful.
Medical Bills Are Not.

Costa Rica has excellent private hospitals, but they operate on a cash-and-insurance basis. A night of hospitalization can run $1,500–$4,000 USD. An emergency medical flight can exceed $30,000. A broken ankle on a volcano hike or a severe allergic reaction during a rainforest tour becomes not just a health crisis, but a financial one, unless you're covered.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I really need travel insurance if I already have health insurance at home?

Most domestic health insurance plans in the US and Canada do not cover international medical care, or cover only a small fraction of actual costs. Costa Rica's private hospitals require direct payment or insurance confirmation. Your home policy is very unlikely to cover a medical evacuation flight, which alone can exceed $30,000 USD.

When should I purchase travel insurance?

Ideally before you board your flight. Some trip cancellation benefits apply only if you purchase within a specific window after booking your flights, so earlier is better. Medical coverage activates from the day you arrive in Costa Rica.

Does Assist Card cover adventure activities like zip-lining and volcano hiking?

Selected Assist Card plans include coverage for adventure and outdoor activities. We'll confirm which plan covers the specific activities on your itinerary before you purchase.

What if I have a pre-existing medical condition?

Assist Card offers plans that cover stable pre-existing conditions. Requirements vary by plan. We recommend sharing this information with us when requesting your quote so we can match you to the right option.

How does Assist Card work in an actual emergency?

You call the 24/7 Assist Card emergency hotline (printed on your policy card). They coordinate directly with the hospital, authorize coverage, arrange transfers if needed, and guide your family in English, Spanish and other languages. Zero deductible means no upfront cost to you.

How long does it take to get covered after I contact you?

We typically send your quote within the hour and can have your policy documents delivered by email the same day. For last-minute trips, same-day coverage is often possible.

Can I buy insurance after I've already arrived in Costa Rica?

In most cases, yes: Assist Card can be purchased after arrival, though some benefits like trip cancellation only apply when purchased before your trip begins. Contact us as soon as possible and we'll confirm what's available for your situation.

Does travel insurance cover rental cars?

Assist Card plans cover personal accident and medical expenses while in or around a rental vehicle, but physical damage to the car itself is typically handled through your credit card's collision benefit or an add-on. We can advise you on what gaps exist based on your specific plan.
