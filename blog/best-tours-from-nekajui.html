Best Tours From Nekajui: Day Trips by Pace and Distance

September 21, 2026 ·6 min read · Wild Papagayo Local Team

Updated September 2026

Which day trips are worth leaving Nekajui, A Ritz-Carlton Reserve for? Six private tours sorted by pace and measured driving time, from wildlife and volcano hikes to waterfalls and adventure.

Quick Facts
  • Nekajui, A Ritz-Carlton Reserve is on Peninsula Papagayo, about 36–46 minutes (34.5 km) from Liberia airport (LIR) by private transfer
  • Driving time from Nekajui: Buena Vista 91–116 min, Rincón (Las Pailas) 97–124 min, Bijagua 100–128 min, Río Celeste 114–146 min
  • Arenal and La Fortuna are about 176–224 minutes of driving from Nekajui and a very long day
  • The six tours in this guide run about 8 to 9 hours
  • Rincón de la Vieja hiking runs Tuesday to Sunday because the national park is closed on Mondays
  • Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed on any tour

Private tours are available with pickup directly from Nekajui, A Ritz-Carlton Reserve, but the useful question is less which tour is best than how far you want to drive and what kind of day you want. Several strong options sit roughly 1.5 to 2.5 hours from Nekajui by road, so each one is a full day. This guide sorts six of Wild Papagayo's private tours by pace and by measured driving time from Nekajui, where we have it. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed on any tour.

Wildlife & Forest: Hanging Bridges & Sloths

A gentle way to combine forest and wildlife in one day: about 9 hours, rated Easy, with three suspension bridges, the Llanos de Cortez Waterfall and a sloth encounter, and lunch included. It suits groups with mixed abilities. We have no Nekajui drive time for this one, so we do not quote one; confirm timing when you book. The cost is a full day, and no tour can promise a sighting. Sloths + Culture: Sloth Adventure & Chocolate

An Easy day of about 8 hours built around a chocolate experience that follows cacao from tree to handmade bar, and a short forest walk in Bijagua looking for sloths, butterflies and toucans, lunch included. Driving time from Nekajui to the Bijagua area is about 100 to 128 minutes; that is the drive to the area, not an exact tour transfer time. The tradeoff is that drive, and sloth sightings are never guaranteed. View the tour →

Volcano + Relaxation: Miravalles Volcano & Natural Spa

A soak-and-scenery day at Miravalles Volcano: three waterfalls, geothermal hot springs, volcanic mud treatments and some hiking over about 8 hours (rated Easy to Moderate), with lunch and entrance fees covered. It suits travelers who want to unwind. We have not specifically measured the drive from Nekajui, so treat it as a full-day option. The tradeoff is that there is no measured drive time to plan around. For other waterfall days, try our waterfall comparison. View the tour →

Volcano Hiking: Rincón de la Vieja Hiking Tour

Trails through Rincón de la Vieja National Park fill about 8 hours, rated Easy to Moderate, past volcanic fumaroles and a waterfall with wildlife possible along the way; lunch is included. Driving time from Nekajui to the Las Pailas area is about 97 to 124 minutes. The catch is timing: the park closes on Mondays, so this tour runs Tuesday to Sunday only. Our Rincón day-trip guide compares the tour formats. View the tour →

Adventure Day: Buena Vista Mega Combo

Zip lining (seven cables), a waterslide, horseback riding and hot springs share one Moderate-rated day of about 8 hours, with lunch included. Driving time from Nekajui to the Buena Vista Adventure Center is about 91 to 116 minutes, one of the two lower measured ranges here. It suits active groups that want variety rather than one long hike. The catch is pace: the day is full. View the tour →

Scenic Long Day: Celeste River Hike

The hike to Río Celeste inside Tenorio Volcano National Park fills about 9 hours and is rated Moderate; lunch and the park fee are included. Driving time from Nekajui to Río Celeste is about 114 to 146 minutes, the longest of the measured drives. Expect about 6 km round trip over stairs and uneven ground, with the river itself off-limits for swimming. Visitor identification details are needed for park registration. Our Río Celeste guide weighs whether it is worth the drive. View the tour →

Local Expert Tip

If you are unsure how much driving you want, start with the lower measured ranges, Buena Vista and Rincón, and decide from there whether a longer day like Río Celeste appeals.

How Far Do You Want to Go?

Every option here is a full day, so the decision is how much of it you spend on the road. The ranges overlap, so read them as a rough guide.

  • Around 1.5 to 2 hours: Buena Vista (91–116 minutes), Rincón de la Vieja (97–124 minutes) and the Bijagua area for Sloth Adventure & Chocolate (100–128 minutes).
  • Around 2 hours or a little more: Río Celeste (114–146 minutes).
  • No Nekajui-specific measurement: Hanging Bridges & Sloths and Miravalles. Ask us when you book.

Very Long Days

Arenal and La Fortuna are the outer edge: about 176 to 224 minutes of driving from Nekajui, with tours of about 12 hours. Monteverde is not measured from Nekajui; the general Papagayo-area figure is about 3 hours each way and a 12-hour day, covered in our Monteverde guide.

Which Day Fits Your Trip?

  • Wildlife: Hanging Bridges & Sloths or Sloth Adventure & Chocolate. Both are rated Easy, which also makes them natural choices for mixed groups.
  • Relaxation: Miravalles, for waterfalls and hot springs.
  • Adventure: Buena Vista for variety, or the Rincón hike for a walking-focused day.
  • Less driving: Buena Vista and Rincón have the lowest measured ranges.
  • A longer scenic day: Río Celeste, if you are comfortable with the drive and a hike.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best tours from Nekajui?

That depends on the kind of day you want. Wild Papagayo's private options from Nekajui include Hanging Bridges and Sloths (wildlife and forest), Sloth Adventure and Chocolate (sloths and culture), Miravalles (volcano and relaxation), the Rincón de la Vieja hike (volcano hiking), Buena Vista Mega Combo (adventure) and the Celeste River Hike (a scenic long day).

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly at Nekajui?

Yes. Private tours start with pickup directly at Nekajui, and the departure time depends on the tour you choose.

Are private tours available from Nekajui?

Yes. Every tour in this guide is a private Wild Papagayo tour from Nekajui, with private transportation and a certified bilingual guide.

Is Rincón de la Vieja worth visiting from Nekajui?

Worth it if volcano scenery appeals and you are comfortable with a full day. Driving time from Nekajui to the Las Pailas area is about 97 to 124 minutes, and the Rincón de la Vieja Hiking Tour runs about 8 hours, Tuesday through Sunday, because the park is closed on Mondays.

Can you see sloths on a tour from Nekajui?

Yes, but sloth tours go into forest areas rather than the resort coast. Sloth Adventure and Chocolate visits Bijagua, about 100 to 128 minutes of driving from Nekajui, and Hanging Bridges and Sloths runs about 9 hours; both are rated Easy. A guide helps you search, but no tour can guarantee a sighting.

Is Río Celeste a good day trip from Nekajui?

Yes, for travelers who are happy with a long day and a proper hike. Driving time from Nekajui to Río Celeste is about 114 to 146 minutes, and the Celeste River Hike runs about 9 hours and is rated Moderate. Expect about 6 km round trip on the trail, with no swimming in the river. Visitor identification details are needed for park registration.

Is Arenal too far for a day trip from Nekajui?

It is a very long day. Driving time from Nekajui to La Fortuna and Arenal is about 176 to 224 minutes and Arenal tours run about 12 hours, while the measured options in this guide are all closer. It suits travelers who specifically want Arenal.
