Quick Facts

Private Transportation From Liberia Airport

For families, couples, and groups, private transportation is usually the easiest option. Your driver meets you at the designated arrivals pickup area with a personalized sign, assists with luggage, and takes you directly to your hotel without additional stops.

This option is especially popular for travelers staying in Papagayo, Riu Guanacaste, Secrets Papagayo, Four Seasons, Andaz, Tamarindo, Playa Hermosa, Playa Flamingo, and surrounding beaches.

Renting a Car at Liberia Airport

Renting a car gives you flexibility, but it's not always the best value. Besides the rental price, visitors should consider mandatory insurance, fuel, parking fees, and driving on unfamiliar roads.

If your vacation mainly includes guided tours and relaxing at your resort, many travelers discover they only use the rental car once or twice.

Local Tip
Many resorts in Guanacaste include transportation for excursions or offer pickup services. If you're planning only two or three adventure days, private transfers can actually cost less than renting a vehicle for the entire week.

Liberia Airport Shared Shuttle Services

Shared shuttles are a budget-friendly alternative but typically involve waiting for other passengers and making several hotel stops before reaching your destination. If you're arriving after a long international flight, the extra travel time can be significant.

What Happens When You Arrive at LIR?

After collecting your luggage and clearing customs, private transfer guests head to the designated arrivals pickup area, where your driver will be waiting with a personalized sign. From there, it's a direct trip to your hotel with no additional stops unless you've requested one.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Papagayo from Liberia Airport?

Most resorts in the Papagayo Peninsula are about 30 to 45 minutes from Liberia International Airport, depending on the exact property.

What is the best transportation from Liberia Airport?

For most families, couples and groups, a pre-booked private transfer is the easiest option: a driver meets you at arrivals and takes you directly to your hotel with no extra stops.

Are private transfers available from Liberia Airport?

Yes. Private transfers are available to Papagayo, Playa Hermosa, Playa del Coco, Tamarindo, Flamingo and other Guanacaste destinations.

Is there a shuttle from Liberia Airport to Papagayo?

Shared shuttles are available as a budget-friendly option, though they typically involve waiting for other passengers and multiple hotel stops before reaching Papagayo.

Should I book Liberia Airport transportation in advance?

Yes. Booking in advance guarantees availability and eliminates the uncertainty of finding transportation after landing.

Can my driver meet me if my flight is delayed?

Private transfers typically track your flight and adjust the pickup time accordingly. Let your provider know your flight number in advance so this can be arranged.

Can I request a child seat?

Most private transfer providers can arrange a child seat if requested in advance. Mention the age of your child when booking.

Is Uber available in Liberia?

Uber operates in some areas of Costa Rica, but availability at Liberia Airport is inconsistent. Most visitors choose pre-arranged transportation for convenience.

Can I stop at a supermarket on the way to my hotel?

Absolutely. Most private transfers can include a grocery stop upon request, allowing you to stock up on snacks, drinks, or other essentials before arriving at your resort.
