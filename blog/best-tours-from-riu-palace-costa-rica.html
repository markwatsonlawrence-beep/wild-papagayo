Best Tours From Riu Palace Costa Rica: Which Day Trips Are Worth It?

Updated September 2026

Which day trips are actually worth leaving Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica for? Seven private tours sorted by what you want from the day: wildlife, families, adventure, volcano hiking, relaxation, a shorter option or a scenic long day.

Quick Facts
  • Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica is about 41–53 minutes (33.2 km) from Liberia airport (LIR) by private transfer
  • The seven tours in this guide run from about 6 hours to about 9 hours
  • Waterfall and Las Pumas is the shortest option at about 6 hours
  • Rincón de la Vieja hiking runs Tuesday to Sunday because the national park is closed on Mondays
  • Travel times to Rincón (about 102–130 minutes) and Río Celeste (about 2–2.5 hours) are each way from the Papagayo area
  • Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed on any tour

Private tours are available from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica, with pickup at the resort, and the right one depends on how much of your day you want to spend away from it. Wildlife, waterfalls, volcanoes and adventure are all practical from Guanacaste, but some destinations mean a much longer day than others. This guide sorts seven of Wild Papagayo's private tours by what you want from the day, from a shorter half day to a scenic full one. Wildlife sightings are never guaranteed on any tour.

Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica and Riu Guanacaste are separate properties in the Playa Matapalo area. If you are staying at Riu Guanacaste instead, start with our guide to tours from Riu Guanacaste.

Best for Wildlife: Hanging Bridges & Sloths

An Easy, full-day tour of about 9 hours with three suspension bridges, the Llanos de Cortez Waterfall and a sloth encounter, lunch included. It suits mixed-ability groups and keeps the day focused on a Guanacaste experience. The tradeoff is a full day away, and wildlife is never guaranteed. See our sloth tour comparison. View the tour →

Best for Families: Sloth Adventure & Chocolate

An Easy day of about 8 hours: a hands-on chocolate experience, from cacao tree to handmade bar, then a short forest walk in Bijagua in search of sloths, butterflies and toucans, lunch included. The relaxed pace suits families. The tradeoff is that Bijagua is inland, so the wildlife search takes you inland, and sightings are never guaranteed. View the tour →

Best Adventure Day: Buena Vista Mega Combo

A Moderate, full-day adventure of about 8 hours in Guanacaste: a 7-cable zip line, waterslide, horseback riding and hot springs, lunch included. It fits active groups who want several activities in one day. The tradeoff is pace, since it is a busy, active day. View the tour →

Best Volcano Hike: Rincón de la Vieja Hiking Tour

About 8 hours in Rincón de la Vieja National Park, rated Easy to Moderate, with volcanic fumaroles, a waterfall and wildlife along the trails, lunch included. It suits travelers who want volcano scenery and a real walk. The tradeoff is the calendar: the park is closed on Mondays, so the tour runs Tuesday through Sunday, and the drive is roughly 102 to 130 minutes each way from the Papagayo area. See our Rincón de la Vieja day-trip guide. View the tour →

Best for Relaxation: Miravalles Volcano Waterfalls & Natural Spa

About 8 hours at Miravalles Volcano, rated Easy to Moderate: three waterfalls, geothermal hot springs, volcanic mud treatments and some hiking, with lunch and entrance fees included. It suits travelers who want to unwind. The tradeoff is that it is still a full day away from the resort. View the tour →

Best Shorter Day: Waterfall & Las Pumas Wildlife Rescue Center

The shortest of the seven, about 6 hours and rated Easy: a swim at the Llanos del Cortes waterfall, then Las Pumas Wildlife Rescue Center to meet rescued pumas, jaguars, monkeys and macaws, lunch included. It suits groups who want a half day with water and wildlife. The tradeoffs: the waterfall runs fullest from May to November, and a rescue center differs from spotting wildlife in the forest. See our waterfall day-trip comparison. View the tour →

Best Scenic Long Day: Celeste River Hike

A Moderate, full-day hike of about 9 hours to Río Celeste in Tenorio Volcano National Park, with lunch and the park fee included. It suits travelers who are happy to walk for a scenic long day. The tradeoff is the drive, roughly 2 to 2.5 hours each way from the Papagayo area, plus a trail of about 6 km round trip with stairs and uneven ground. Swimming is not allowed in the river. See whether Río Celeste is worth the drive. View the tour →

Local Expert Tip

Rincón de la Vieja National Park is closed on Mondays, so plan the volcano hiking day for Tuesday through Sunday.

How Much Day Do You Want?

These are approximate experience lengths; real timing depends on the tour and your pickup.

  • Shorter, around 6 hours: Waterfall & Las Pumas.
  • Full day, around 8–9 hours: the other six tours above.
  • Long day, around 10 hours: multi-stop private days combining several highlights.
  • Very long, around 12 hours: Monteverde and Arenal-style days.

Travel Times From the Papagayo Area

Tour travel times are from the Papagayo area, not measured from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica itself, so treat them as a guide.

  • Liberia airport (LIR): about 41–53 minutes (33.2 km) from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica by private transfer. See our Liberia airport guide.
  • Rincón de la Vieja: approximately 102–130 minutes each way.
  • Río Celeste: approximately 2 to 2.5 hours each way.
  • Monteverde: roughly 3 hours each way, about a 12-hour day. See our Monteverde guide.
  • Arenal and La Fortuna: a very long day of about 12 hours.

Which Tour Fits Your Group?

  • Families: start with the Easy-rated days: the two sloth tours or, for less time away, Waterfall & Las Pumas.
  • Wildlife: the two sloth tours, plus a rescue-center visit at Las Pumas. For turtle nesting, see our Ostional turtle guide.
  • Adventure: Buena Vista for several activities, or the Rincón hike if you would rather walk.
  • Relaxation: Miravalles for waterfalls and hot springs.

When Private Transportation Matters

Every tour here includes private transportation and a certified bilingual guide, with pickup directly from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica and no separate meeting point. That matters most on long Guanacaste days, when coordinating separate transfers is real effort. Departure times depend on the tour. See also things to do in Papagayo.

What About Leaving the Resort Without a Tour?

Not every day away from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica has to be one of the seven tours above. For travelers who want a shorter change of scene, nearby beach towns such as Playas del Coco and Tamarindo are both an option. Wild Papagayo can arrange private round-trip transportation to either on request — this is transportation, not a packaged excursion, so the itinerary once you arrive is up to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best tours from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

It depends on the day you want. Wild Papagayo's private options from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica cover wildlife (Hanging Bridges and Sloths), families (Sloth Adventure and Chocolate), adventure (Buena Vista Mega Combo), a volcano hike (Rincón de la Vieja), relaxation (Miravalles), a shorter day (Waterfall and Las Pumas) and a scenic long day (Celeste River Hike).

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly at Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Yes. Private tours pick you up at Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica itself, with no separate meeting point.

Are private tours available from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Yes. Wild Papagayo offers private tours from Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica to waterfalls, wildlife areas, volcanoes and adventure destinations across Guanacaste, with private transportation and a certified bilingual guide.

Is Rincón de la Vieja worth visiting from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Yes, if you want volcano scenery and are happy with a full day. Rincón de la Vieja is approximately 102 to 130 minutes each way from the Papagayo area, depending on traffic and your pickup. The hiking tour runs about 8 hours, Tuesday through Sunday, because the park is closed on Mondays.

Can you see sloths on a tour from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Yes, but sloth tours head into forest areas away from the beach. Hanging Bridges and Sloths (about 9 hours) and Sloth Adventure and Chocolate (about 8 hours) are both rated Easy. A certified guide helps you look, but sloth sightings are never guaranteed.

Which tours work well for families staying at Riu Palace Costa Rica?

The Easy-rated options are the natural starting point: Hanging Bridges and Sloths (about 9 hours), Sloth Adventure and Chocolate (about 8 hours) and, for a shorter day, Waterfall and Las Pumas (about 6 hours).

Is Río Celeste a good day trip from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

It can be, if you are comfortable with a long day and a hike. Río Celeste is approximately 2 to 2.5 hours each way from the Papagayo area, and the Celeste River Hike runs about 9 hours and is rated Moderate. The trail is about 6 km round trip and swimming is not allowed. Visitor identification details are required for park registration.

Can I go surfing from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Surfing isn't part of the tours in this guide, but Tamarindo is a well-known surf-oriented beach town if that fits your trip. Wild Papagayo can arrange private round-trip transportation there on request.

Is there nightlife near Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica offers its own on-site evening entertainment for guests who prefer to stay on property. For those who want to leave the resort in the evening, Playas del Coco and Tamarindo are both nearby options, and Wild Papagayo can arrange private transportation there on request.

Which Riu Palace Costa Rica tours work for young children?

Several of these tours have no stated minimum age and are rated Easy: Hanging Bridges & Sloths, Sloth Adventure & Chocolate, and the shorter Waterfall & Las Pumas, about 6 hours. Buena Vista Mega Combo and the Celeste River Hike both have a minimum age of 8.

Is there a gentler day for grandparents or travelers who prefer less walking?

Waterfall & Las Pumas is the shortest at about 6 hours and rated Easy. Hanging Bridges & Sloths and Sloth Adventure & Chocolate are also rated Easy, though both run closer to a full day of 8 to 9 hours. Rincón de la Vieja Hiking and Miravalles are rated Easy to Moderate with real walking involved; Buena Vista Mega Combo and the Celeste River Hike are rated Moderate, more physical and run the longest. Travelers who prefer less walking may want to compare these before booking.

Do we need to book a tour every day of our stay?

No. Most of these tours run 6 to 9 hours, so pairing a tour day with a resort or rest day works well for many multigenerational groups, particularly with grandparents or young children along.

Should we book earlier if we are visiting in December or during high season?

December falls within Guanacaste's dry season, which is also the region's busiest travel period, so booking your preferred tour dates a bit earlier is a reasonable precaution rather than leaving it to the last minute.
