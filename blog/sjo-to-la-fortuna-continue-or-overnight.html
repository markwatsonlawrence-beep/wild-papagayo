SJO to La Fortuna: Continue Today or Stay Overnight?

September 23, 2026 ·6 min read · Wild Papagayo Local Team

Updated September 2026

Landing at SJO in the afternoon or evening? Here's how to decide whether to continue to La Fortuna the same day or stay near the airport and travel the next morning.

Quick Facts
  • Wild Papagayo can arrange private transportation from SJO to La Fortuna/Arenal on request
  • After-4:00 p.m. arrivals: Wild Papagayo generally recommends an overnight stay near SJO
  • No airport-to-La-Fortuna transfer continues for flights arriving at night
  • Rental-car travelers get more conservative same-day guidance than private-driver travelers
  • The 4:00 p.m. threshold is a planning recommendation, not a safety or legal rule

The Short Answer

If your flight lands at SJO in the afternoon, before about 4:00 p.m., continuing to La Fortuna that day is usually workable once you're through immigration, baggage claim and, if you're renting, the rental-car counter. If your flight lands after 4:00 p.m., Wild Papagayo generally recommends staying overnight near the SJO/San José airport area and continuing to La Fortuna the next morning. That isn't a claim that driving after 4:00 p.m. is unsafe — it's a planning recommendation based on how the rest of your arrival day actually unfolds, and it can shift depending on your transport mode and how quickly you clear the airport. If your flight arrives at night, Wild Papagayo does not continue the airport transfer to La Fortuna that same night, regardless of what time you landed.

Landing Time Isn't Departure Time

The time on your boarding pass is when the plane touches down, not when you'll actually be on the road. Immigration, baggage claim, and, if you're renting a car, the rental counter and vehicle paperwork all happen first, and none of those move at a fixed, predictable pace — a quiet afternoon at SJO looks very different from a delayed evening with several flights landing close together. That gap is exactly why a flat rule based on scheduled arrival time alone doesn't work well: two travelers who both land at 3:00 p.m. can be back on the road well apart depending on the line at immigration or how long the rental paperwork takes.

Local Expert Tip

Treat your flight's scheduled landing time as the start of the clock, not the moment you'll be on the road — immigration, baggage and any rental-car pickup all happen first.

What If Your Flight Arrives After 4:00 P.M.?

For flights arriving after 4:00 p.m., Wild Papagayo generally recommends staying overnight in the San José/SJO airport area and continuing to La Fortuna the following morning. This is a planning recommendation, not a safety verdict — it accounts for the realistic time it takes to clear the airport, get on the road, and drive an unfamiliar route for the first time, on top of an already long travel day. It isn't a guarantee that every arrival before 4:00 p.m. should continue, either: a long delay, a slow immigration line, or simply feeling too tired after an overnight flight can make waiting until morning the better call even earlier in the day. Treat 4:00 p.m. as a general planning marker, not a countdown clock.

Rental Car vs. Private Driver: Why It Changes the Calculus

If You're Collecting a Rental Car

Travelers who still need to complete a rental-car pickup after landing are the ones Wild Papagayo takes the most conservative approach with. On top of airport processing, you're also handling paperwork, inspecting the vehicle, and then personally driving an unfamiliar route for the first time, often in the same stretch of hours the light is fading. That combination is why the after-4:00 p.m. guidance tends to apply most firmly to self-drive travelers.

If You're Using a Private Driver

With a private driver, there's more flexibility, because you're not the one who has to navigate or drive. That said, this flexibility doesn't override Wild Papagayo's night-arrival policy below — a private driver changes how comfortable the drive is, not whether the transfer runs after dark. Actual conditions on the day, including how late the flight actually lands, still factor into the final call.

What Changes With a Night Arrival

Regardless of transport mode, Wild Papagayo does not continue the airport-to-La-Fortuna transfer for flights arriving at night. This mirrors the same policy already in place for Liberia Airport (LIR) transfers to La Fortuna & Arenal, kept consistent across both airports rather than treating San José differently. There's no fixed clock time attached to "night" beyond the 4:00 p.m. planning threshold above — it reflects the practical reality of arriving very late, in the dark, on a route you likely haven't driven before.

When Staying Near SJO Makes Sense

Beyond the 4:00 p.m. guidance, a few other things can tip the decision toward staying near the airport for the first night: a long or delayed international flight, traveling with young children who are past their limit for one more stretch in the car, or simply not wanting your first hours in Costa Rica to be spent navigating in the dark. None of these are hard rules — they're the kind of traveler-comfort factors worth weighing alongside the arrival-time guidance above.

What the Next Morning Looks Like

An overnight stay near SJO isn't a lost day — it's simply a reshuffled one. Most travelers who stay near the airport head to La Fortuna the next morning with a full day already ahead of them, arriving in daylight and without the fatigue of a same-day continuation. It also gives a buffer if your flight or bags were delayed, since you're not racing a clock to reach La Fortuna before dark. Frequently Asked Questions

Should I travel from SJO to La Fortuna after an afternoon flight?

In many cases, yes — if you land before about 4:00 p.m. and clear the airport reasonably quickly, continuing the same day is usually workable. This depends on your actual airport-exit time and transport mode, not just your scheduled landing time.

What if my flight arrives after 4:00 p.m.?

Wild Papagayo generally recommends staying overnight near the SJO/San José airport area and continuing to La Fortuna the next morning. This is a planning recommendation based on how the rest of arrival day unfolds, not a statement that driving after 4:00 p.m. is unsafe.

Can I drive from SJO to La Fortuna at night?

Wild Papagayo does not continue the airport-to-La-Fortuna transfer for flights arriving at night, the same policy already used for Liberia Airport (LIR) transfers. This is Wild Papagayo's own transfer policy, not a general statement about driving safety in Costa Rica.

Is a private driver different from renting a car after landing?

Yes. With a private driver, there's more flexibility since you're not the one navigating or driving, while travelers collecting a rental car face a more conservative recommendation because they still have to complete pickup and then drive the route themselves. Either way, the night-arrival policy still applies.

Should I stay near SJO overnight instead of continuing?

It can be the better choice for late or delayed flights, travelers with young children, or anyone who would rather not spend their first hours in Costa Rica driving after dark. It's a comfort-based decision as much as a timing one.

Does Wild Papagayo provide SJO to La Fortuna transportation?

Yes, Wild Papagayo can arrange private transportation from SJO to La Fortuna/Arenal on request.

What should I plan for the morning after an overnight stay near SJO?

Most travelers head to La Fortuna the next morning with a full day ahead, arriving in daylight and without the fatigue of a same-day continuation right after an international flight.
