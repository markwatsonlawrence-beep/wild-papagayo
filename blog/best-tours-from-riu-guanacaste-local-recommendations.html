Staying at Hotel Riu Guanacaste and looking for the best tours and excursions? Wild Papagayo offers private tours with direct hotel pickup to some of Guanacaste's top destinations, including Río Celeste, wildlife reserves, waterfalls, hanging bridges and adventure parks. These are the private tours we most often recommend to Riu Guanacaste guests based on travel time, activity level and overall experience.

What Are the Best Tours From Riu Guanacaste?

Hotel Riu Guanacaste sits on Playa Matapalo, roughly a 42–54 minute drive from Liberia airport (LIR), which puts it within easy private-transfer reach of Guanacaste's best day trips. Over the years we've sent Riu guests on all of these, and four stand out: the Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience, the Celeste River Hike at Tenorio, the Palo Verde River Boat Tour, and the Buena Vista Mega Combo.

The right pick depends on your pace. The hanging bridges and sloth day (about 9 hours, rated Easy) suits families and mixed-ability groups. Río Celeste and Buena Vista are Moderate and more physical. Palo Verde is the shortest at roughly 6 hours and is rated Easy, a good fit if you want a wildlife outing that takes less than a full day.

Our Recommendation

If it's your first time in Guanacaste, we usually steer Riu guests toward the Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience — three suspension bridges through tropical forest, Llanos de Cortez Waterfall, an intimate sloth encounter, and lunch included. It's the easiest to enjoy for every age and consistently the one guests thank us for.

Local Expert Tip

Palo Verde's boat tour is the shortest of the four at about 6 hours, but its departure time is set according to the river tide, so confirm your pickup time before planning anything else that day.

How to Plan It Well

Because every tour we run from Riu is private, you set the departure time and pace — no shared vans, no fixed schedules. That matters at an all-inclusive resort, where you'll want to be back in time for dinner and a drink at one of the seven bars. Full-day tours run 8–9 hours, so a private start lets you leave early and beat the heat and crowds.

The Celeste River Hike is the one that trips people up: it requires passport details for every guest, since the national park logs entries. Send those to us in advance so there are no delays at the gate.

Local Expert Tip
Book Río Celeste for the December–April dry season. The aquamarine color is at its most vivid when rains haven't stirred up the riverbed, and the trail footing is far better on dry ground.

Our Bottom Line

You can't go wrong with any of these four, but match the tour to your group: sloths and hanging bridges for an easy, universally loved day; Río Celeste or Buena Vista if you want something more active; and Palo Verde's half-day boat tour if you'd rather have the shortest outing of the four. All are private with certified bilingual guides — message Wild Papagayo on WhatsApp and we'll help you choose and lock in your times.

Beyond These Tours: Nearby Towns

Not every day away from the resort needs to be a full inland excursion. If you want a change of scene closer to the coast, beach towns like Playas del Coco and Tamarindo are both within reach. Wild Papagayo can arrange private round-trip transportation to either on request — this is transportation only, not a packaged sightseeing tour, so what you do once you're there is entirely up to you.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best tours from Riu Guanacaste?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience, Río Celeste Hike, Palo Verde River Boat Tour and Buena Vista Mega Combo are the four we most often recommend, based on years of arranging trips from the resort.

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly from Riu Guanacaste?

Yes. Every tour includes private pickup directly from Hotel Riu Guanacaste, with no separate meeting point to reach on your own.

What is the best Riu Guanacaste tour for families?

The Hanging Bridges & Sloths Experience is rated Easy and includes lunch, three suspension bridges, a waterfall and a sloth encounter that appeals to every age.

Can I visit Río Celeste from Riu Guanacaste?

Yes. It's a full-day private tour, roughly 9 hours round trip, best booked in the December–April dry season for the most vivid water color.

Are the tours private or shared?

All Wild Papagayo tours from Riu Guanacaste are private for your group, with your own guide, vehicle and pace, not a shared van with other travelers.

How far is Riu Guanacaste from Liberia Airport?

About 42–54 minutes by private transfer from Liberia International Airport (LIR).

Can I customize a private tour from Riu Guanacaste?

Yes. Since every tour is private, departure time and pacing can be adjusted to your group. Message us on WhatsApp with your dates and group size and we'll help you plan it.

Do I need my passport for any of these tours?

Yes — the Celeste River Hike & Tenorio National Park tour requires passport details for all guests because the national park records entries. Send them to us ahead of time to avoid delays at the entrance.

Can I go surfing while staying at Riu Guanacaste?

Surfing isn't one of the private tours in this guide, but Tamarindo is a well-known surf-oriented beach town within reach if that's part of your plans. Wild Papagayo can arrange private round-trip transportation there on request.

Is there nightlife near Riu Guanacaste?

Riu Guanacaste has its own on-site evening entertainment and seven bars for guests who'd rather stay on property. If you want to head out for the evening instead, Playas del Coco and Tamarindo are both options, and Wild Papagayo can arrange private transportation there on request.

Which Riu Guanacaste tours work for young children?

Palo Verde River Boat has a minimum age of 2 and is a seated riverboat cruise of about 6 hours, the most practical option with young children. Hanging Bridges & Sloths has no stated minimum age but is a full day of about 9 hours with walking across suspension bridges. Río Celeste and Buena Vista Mega Combo both have a minimum age of 8.

Is there a gentler option for grandparents or travelers who prefer less walking?

Palo Verde River Boat is the gentlest of the four: Easy-rated, seated the entire time, and the shortest at about 6 hours. Hanging Bridges & Sloths is also rated Easy but involves walking across suspension bridges over a full day of about 9 hours. Río Celeste and Buena Vista Mega Combo are rated Moderate, more physical and run longer. Travelers who prefer less walking may want to compare these before booking.

Do we need to book a tour every day of our stay?

No. Full-day tours run 8 to 9 hours, so many multigenerational groups pair one or two tour days with resort or rest days in between, especially with grandparents or young children in the group.

Should we book earlier if we are visiting in December or during high season?

December falls within Guanacaste's dry season, which is also the region's busiest travel period, so booking your preferred tour dates a bit earlier is a reasonable precaution rather than leaving it to the last minute.
