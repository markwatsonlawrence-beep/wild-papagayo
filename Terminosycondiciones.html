Booking Confirmation

All bookings must be confirmed in writing via WhatsApp or email. Payments

Bookings are confirmed with a 25% deposit. The remaining 75% is due no later than 72 hours before the service begins, and bookings made within 72 hours require full payment at the time of booking. Our standard electronic payment method is Compra Click (BAC); we don't add a card surcharge. Cash may be accepted only when we offer it in writing for a specific booking. Some multi-day journeys and special services have their own terms, confirmed in writing before payment. Prices quoted are in US Dollars unless stated otherwise.

All payment instructions are confirmed directly by our team before any transaction is completed.

Common Questions

Can I transfer my booking to another person?
Yes, bookings may be transferred to another traveler, subject to our confirmation. Please let us know in advance via WhatsApp so we can update the reservation details.
What happens if my flight is delayed?
For airport transfers, we monitor your flight in real time and adjust your pickup accordingly at no extra charge. For tours, please notify us as soon as possible and we will do our best to accommodate the delay.
Can I reschedule instead of canceling?
Absolutely — rescheduling is always our first preference. If your plans change, reach out more than 72 hours before your service and we'll do our best to find a new date, subject to availability. Within 72 hours, changes are exceptional and at our discretion.
Are deposits refundable?
Deposit refundability depends on when you cancel. Cancellations made more than 72 hours before the service receive the refundable amount paid, less any applicable non-recoverable payment-processing costs when legally permitted; cancellations within 72 hours are non-refundable, subject to any mandatory consumer rights.
