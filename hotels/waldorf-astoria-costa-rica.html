Our local insight

Waldorf Astoria Punta Cacique sits right on Playa Penca, with a 17,000-square-foot spa, a cascading multi-level pool and six on-site restaurants and bars, from the signature La Finca to Copo y Cono for ice cream. It suits travelers who value privacy, ocean views and a short arrival day, with easy private transfers to explore Playa Hermosa and Playas del Coco nearby.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica from Guanacaste Airport?

The resort is approximately 17 miles from Guanacaste Airport (LIR). Driving time is commonly around 25 to 35 minutes, depending on traffic.

Where is Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica located?

The resort is located at Punta Cacique on Playa Penca, along Guanacaste's Northern Pacific coast.

Can tours pick up at Waldorf Astoria Punta Cacique?

Private excursions and transfers can be planned from the resort, with pickup details confirmed according to the selected experience and travel date.
