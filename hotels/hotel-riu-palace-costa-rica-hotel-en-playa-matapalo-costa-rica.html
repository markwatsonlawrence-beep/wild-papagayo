Before you arrive

Frequently asked questions

What room categories does Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica offer?

The resort offers Jr. Suites, connecting Jr. Suites with two rooms, and garden-view Jr. Suites, many designed with sea views, plus fully accessible options.

Is the resort all-inclusive and what's included?

Yes, it's a 24-hour all-inclusive resort with 5 restaurants, 7 bars, six pools, a swim-up bar, spa, gym, and the Splash Water World water park.

Where is Hotel Riu Palace Costa Rica located?

It sits directly on Playa Matapalo near Sardinal in Guanacaste, roughly under an hour from Guanacaste Airport (LIR) in Liberia.

Can I book private tours from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Yes. Wild Papagayo offers private tours with direct hotel pickup from Riu Palace Costa Rica, including waterfalls, wildlife, volcanoes and adventure day trips throughout Guanacaste.

Does Wild Papagayo pick up directly from Riu Palace?

Yes. Private pickup is arranged directly from the resort, so there is no separate meeting point to reach on your own.

How far is Riu Palace Costa Rica from Liberia Airport?

Approximately 41 to 53 minutes by private transfer from Guanacaste Airport (LIR), about 33 km away.

What are the best day trips from Riu Palace Costa Rica?

Popular options include the Buena Vista Mega Combo, Miravalles Volcano, and sloth and wildlife experiences, along with private day trips to Río Celeste and other Guanacaste destinations.
