Hotel Bosque del Mar

Hotel Bosque del Mar is a boutique beachfront hotel on Playa Hermosa, Guanacaste, located about 26–33 minutes from Guanacaste Airport (LIR). Its quiet beach setting, tropical gardens and walkable restaurants make it a convenient base for private tours, airport transportation and day trips throughout Guanacaste.

Frequently asked questions

Which airport should I use for Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Guanacaste Airport (LIR) is the closest practical airport. Private transportation can be arranged directly to the property.

What tours can I book from Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Wild Papagayo can arrange private tours across Guanacaste, with pickup coordinated directly from Hotel Bosque del Mar.

Do I need a rental car if I'm staying at Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Not necessarily. Guests who use organized private tours and transfers can typically manage the trip without renting a car.

Where is Hotel Bosque del Mar located?

Hotel Bosque del Mar sits directly on Playa Hermosa in Guanacaste, Costa Rica, a quiet beach with tropical gardens and walkable restaurants.

How far is Hotel Bosque del Mar from Liberia Airport?

About 26 to 33 minutes by private transfer from Guanacaste Airport (LIR), roughly 24.4 km away.

Can Wild Papagayo pick up directly from Hotel Bosque del Mar?

Yes. Private tour pickup is arranged directly from the hotel, with no separate meeting point to reach on your own.

What are the best day trips from Playa Hermosa?

Popular options include Miravalles Volcano, Monteverde Hanging Bridges and Coffee Tour, and Rincón de la Vieja, all available as private day trips with hotel pickup.
