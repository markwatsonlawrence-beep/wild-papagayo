Why visit Papagayo?

The Papagayo Peninsula is home to some of Costa Rica's most exclusive resort properties, set along calm Pacific bays with easy access to Liberia International Airport. The area combines private service with the natural beauty of Guanacaste.

For travelers who prioritize private, privacy and comfort alongside nature and adventure, Papagayo offers an unmatched base. Private tours to volcanoes, rainforests and wildlife sanctuaries are accessible without long drives.

What travelers usually like

  • Home to Four Seasons, Andaz, Nekajui and other private resorts
  • Calm, protected Pacific bays ideal for swimming and water sports

  • Private and exclusive atmosphere with high-end dining
  • Easy access to Guanacaste's top tours and experiences
  • Ideal for honeymoons, anniversaries and private travel

Things to consider

  • Very resort-focused, limited local dining options outside hotel properties
  • Higher price range than other Guanacaste destinations
  • Less walkable than beach towns like Playa Coco or Tamarindo

Frequently asked questions

Which resorts are located in Papagayo?

The peninsula is home to Four Seasons Resort Costa Rica, Andaz Costa Rica, Nekajui a Ritz-Carlton Reserve, Secrets Papagayo, Planet Hollywood, El Mangroove and other private properties.

How far is Papagayo from Liberia Airport?

Most Papagayo resorts are 30 to 45 minutes from Liberia International Airport by private transfer.

Is Papagayo good for families?

Yes. The calm bays, private resort amenities and family-friendly tour options make Papagayo an excellent choice for families seeking a premium Costa Rica experience.

Can I take day tours from Papagayo?

Absolutely. Private tours to Rincon de la Vieja, hanging bridges, wildlife sanctuaries and other Guanacaste attractions depart easily from the Papagayo area.
