Why visit Rincón de la Vieja?

Rincon de la Vieja National Park is one of Costa Rica's most rewarding adventure destinations. The volcanic terrain combines active geothermal features (bubbling mud pots, hot springs and fumaroles) with dense tropical forest, waterfalls and wildlife.

Most visitors base themselves at nearby haciendas like Buena Vista del Rincon or Hacienda Guachipelin, which offer multi-activity programs combining ziplining, tubing, horseback riding, hot springs and waterfall access in a single day.

Things to consider

  • Inland destination, no beach access from this area
  • Some roads can be rough, especially during the rainy season
  • Best visited with private transportation for easier logistics

Frequently asked questions

Is Rincon de la Vieja suitable for families?

Yes, especially families with older children who enjoy outdoor adventure. Many activities at the haciendas are family-friendly, with options ranging from gentle walks to high-intensity zip lines.

How far is Rincon de la Vieja from Liberia Airport?

Approximately 45 to 75 minutes depending on your specific destination within the area.

What is the best tour to do here?

The Buena Vista Mega Combo and Hacienda Guachipelin multi-adventure programs are among the most popular, combining several activities into one full day.

Can I visit as a day trip from the coast?

Yes. Many travelers staying in Papagayo, Playa Coco or Playa Hermosa do this as a full-day excursion. Private transportation makes the logistics straightforward.
