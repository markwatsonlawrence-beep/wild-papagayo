Why visit Sámara?

Sámara offers an easygoing slice of the Nicoya Peninsula, a low-lying coastal district within the canton of Nicoya in Guanacaste province. With just over 3,500 residents, it retains a small-town, unhurried feel.

Its accessibility improved considerably after the final 35 km of Route 150 from Nicoya through Carrillo was paved in early 2006, and the small Carrillo Airport lies about 5 km east, making the area easier to reach than many peninsula destinations.

CouplesBeach loversRelaxation seekers

What travelers usually like

  • Coastal district on the Nicoya Peninsula sitting at just 3 metres elevation
  • Located about 35 km from Nicoya, the region's economic and administrative hub
  • Small Carrillo Airport roughly 5 km east of town
  • Numerous outlying villages including Playa Buena Vista, Cangrejal and Buenavista
  • Paved road access via National Routes 150, 160 and 934

Things to consider

  • Sámara is a small district of only around 3,500 people, so expect a quiet, low-key atmosphere rather than big-resort amenities.
  • Reaching the area involves a drive of roughly 35 km from Nicoya.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Sámara located?

Sámara is in the Nicoya canton of Guanacaste province, Costa Rica, on the Nicoya Peninsula.

How do I get to Sámara?

The nearest air access is the small Carrillo Airport, about 5 km east of Sámara. By road it lies roughly 35 km from Nicoya via National Route 150, which was fully paved in early 2006.

How big is Sámara?

As of the 2011 census, Sámara had about 3,512 inhabitants, making it a small coastal community.
