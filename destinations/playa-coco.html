Why visit Playas del Coco?

Playas del Coco is one of Guanacaste's most practical beach towns. It combines easy airport access, a walkable center, dozens of restaurants, supermarkets, pharmacies, marina services and a lively social atmosphere that makes it easy to settle in from day one.

Its central location also makes it one of the best bases in Guanacaste for day trips. Most of the province's top attractions (volcanoes, waterfalls, hanging bridges and wildlife sanctuaries) are within a comfortable drive.

Frequently asked questions

How far is Playas del Coco from Liberia Airport?

Approximately 30 minutes by private transfer, making it one of the most convenient beach bases in Guanacaste.

Is Playa Coco good for families?

Yes. The calm bay, walkable town and easy access to tours and services make it an excellent choice for families traveling with children.

What is the best time of year to visit?

December through April is the dry season and offers consistently sunny weather. The Green Season (May to November) brings lower prices, fewer crowds and lush tropical landscapes.

Can I take day tours from Playa Coco?

Absolutely. Coco is one of the best-positioned bases in Guanacaste for day trips to Rincon de la Vieja, hanging bridges, Palo Verde, wildlife sanctuaries and Pacific boat tours.
