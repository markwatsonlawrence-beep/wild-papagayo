Our Story

We didn't start a company. We shared what we loved.

Every road in Costa Rica tells a different story.

Ours began in Guanacaste, sharing hidden places with friends and family long before Wild Papagayo existed. The waterfall no tour bus finds. The local guide who turns a hike into a story. The timing that puts you at the volcano crater at sunrise, alone.

We created Wild Papagayo because travelers kept asking: "How do we find this side of Costa Rica?" Now we show them, personally, privately, and with the same love we've always had for this land.
