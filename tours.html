Private Tours From Papagayo, Guanacaste

Set along the Gulf of Papagayo, this area is one of the best bases for exploring Guanacaste and northern Costa Rica. Private tours allow you to travel directly from your hotel with your own guide and transportation, giving you more flexibility than a shared group excursion.

Wild Papagayo offers private day trips and excursions from Peninsula Papagayo and surrounding resorts to destinations such as Río Celeste, Rincón de la Vieja, Arenal, Palo Verde, waterfalls, wildlife reserves and adventure parks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the best tours from Papagayo, Costa Rica?

It depends on what you want: Río Celeste and Palo Verde for wildlife and scenery, Rincón de la Vieja and Arenal for volcano country, and canyoning, ATV or rafting for adventure. Our tour matcher above can point you to the best fit for your group.

Can you pick us up from a Peninsula Papagayo hotel?

Yes. Private pickup is available from Four Seasons, Andaz, Nekajui, Riu Guanacaste and most hotels and resorts across Papagayo and nearby Guanacaste beach towns.

Are Wild Papagayo tours private?

Yes. Every tour is private for your group, with your own vehicle, guide and pace, not a shared bus with other travelers.

How far is Río Celeste from Papagayo?

Approximately 2 to 2.5 hours by private transfer, which is why our Río Celeste tours run as a full day.

Can families with children book private tours?

Yes. Many of our tours are family-friendly and paced accordingly. Tell us the age of your children when you reach out and we can point you to the tours that fit best.

Can we customize our Papagayo tour?

Yes. Because every tour is private, pickup time, pacing and add-ons can be tailored to your group. Tell us your dates, hotel and interests and we'll help build the day.
