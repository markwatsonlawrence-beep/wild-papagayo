Private Transfer Between Liberia Airport and Peninsula Papagayo

Wild Papagayo arranges private transportation between Liberia Airport (LIR) and Peninsula Papagayo resorts, in both directions. The vehicle and driver are for your group only — not a shared shuttle — and the drive takes approximately 30 to 45 minutes depending on your resort and road conditions.

Liberia Airport is officially Daniel Oduber Quirós International Airport and is also known as Guanacaste Airport. Pricing for this route is by quote: send your dates, direction, group size and flight details on WhatsApp and we will reply with a quote.

Peninsula Papagayo properties served on this route include Four Seasons Resort Costa Rica, Andaz Costa Rica Resort, Nekajui A Ritz-Carlton Reserve, El Mangroove Autograph Collection, Hotel Occidental Papagayo, Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica Guanacaste and Secrets Papagayo Costa Rica. If your property is not listed, include its name when you request your quote.

How It Works

How Your Transfer Works, Step by Step

  1. Send your request on WhatsApp: the direction (airport to Papagayo, or back to the airport), your date, flight details, group size and your resort name.
  2. We check availability and reply with a quote.
  3. If you go ahead, we confirm your booking in writing and arrange a private vehicle and driver for your group.
  4. On arrival at LIR, your driver meets you in the arrivals area with a sign bearing your name. Exact meeting details are confirmed with your booking.
  5. For the return trip, tell us your departure flight time and we plan your pickup for the drive back to Liberia Airport.

Compare Your Options

Private Transfer, Shared Shuttle or Rental Car?

Three common ways to reach Peninsula Papagayo from Liberia Airport.

Private transfer

  • Vehicle and driver for your group only
  • Arranged in both directions on this route
  • Priced by quote for your dates and group
  • Direct to your resort with no shared stops

Shared shuttle

  • Often the budget-friendly choice
  • Typically involves waiting for other passengers and multiple hotel stops before Papagayo

Rental car

  • You set your own schedule and stops
  • You handle the driving and navigation on unfamiliar roads
  • Parking at Papagayo resorts may have fees or limited access

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a private transfer or a shared shuttle?

It is a private transfer. The vehicle and driver are for your group only and are not shared with other travelers.

How long is the drive from Liberia Airport to Peninsula Papagayo?

Approximately 30 to 45 minutes, depending on your resort and road conditions. This is a planning estimate.

How much does a private transfer from Liberia Airport to Papagayo cost?

Pricing is by quote. No fixed price is published for this route. Send your dates, direction, group size and flight details on WhatsApp and we will reply with a quote.

Can I book a round-trip transfer?

Yes. Return transportation from Peninsula Papagayo to Liberia Airport is arranged on this same route. Tell us your departure flight time and resort name when you request your quote.

Which resorts do you serve on Peninsula Papagayo?

Four Seasons Resort Costa Rica, Andaz Costa Rica Resort, Nekajui A Ritz-Carlton Reserve, El Mangroove Autograph Collection, Hotel Occidental Papagayo, Waldorf Astoria Costa Rica Guanacaste and Secrets Papagayo Costa Rica. If your property is not listed, include its name when you request a quote.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

The first 60 minutes of waiting are at no charge. For longer delays we work with you to adjust the plan.

Where will my driver meet me at the airport?

In the arrivals area at LIR, with a sign bearing your name. Exact meeting details are confirmed with your booking.

Can I request a child seat?

You can ask for one when you request your quote, and we will confirm availability.

What should I tell you about my luggage?

Mention your luggage when you request your quote, especially anything unusual or oversized, so we can plan the vehicle accordingly.

How far in advance do I need to book?

There is no fixed lead time; it depends on availability for your dates. Send your request as soon as your plans are set.

What is the cancellation policy for private transfers?

A 25% deposit confirms your transfer and the balance is due no later than 72 hours before pickup; bookings made within 72 hours are paid in full. Cancellations within 72 hours of pickup are non-refundable, subject to any mandatory consumer rights. See our Booking & Cancellation Policy for details.
