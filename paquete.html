Questions

Frequently asked before booking

Can I change the itinerary?

Yes. Every itinerary is a starting point. We adjust activities, add days or completely redesign based on your interests, before or even during the trip.

Can I choose my own hotels?

Absolutely. We can work with your existing hotel bookings or help you choose properties that match your style and budget. Either way, we coordinate all logistics.

Do I have to book every activity?

No. Journeys are flexible by design. Some travelers love a full day-by-day schedule; others prefer breathing room. We build around what works for you.

Can I add transportation to my journey?

Yes. Airport transfers, inter-city routes and daily private transport can be added to any journey. Just let us know your pickup points and we'll build it in.

Can I extend my stay?

Of course. We can extend any journey before or after the main dates, add a beach day, an extra night or a final activity. Just let us know what you're thinking.
