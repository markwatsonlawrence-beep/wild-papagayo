Frequently asked questions

How far is Río Celeste from Papagayo?
Río Celeste, inside Tenorio Volcano National Park, is approximately 2 to 2.5 hours from the Papagayo area by private vehicle, making it a full-day excursion rather than a quick half-day trip.
Is there a closer waterfall option than Río Celeste?
Yes. Llanos del Cortés waterfall is much closer to Papagayo, roughly 45 minutes to an hour, and works well as a half-day trip paired with lunch, making it a good option for groups with young children or limited time.
Which waterfall trip is best for families with young kids?
Llanos del Cortés tends to work better for young children since it's a shorter drive with easy access to swim under the falls. Río Celeste involves a longer hike and drive, which suits older kids and adults better.
Can I combine a waterfall trip with other activities?
Yes. Several of our private tours combine a waterfall with canyoning, tubing, or a stop at a wildlife rescue center, so you can build a fuller day around the drive instead of just visiting one stop.
