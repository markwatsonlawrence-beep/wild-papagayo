The Experience

La Leona is not a waterfall you arrive at directly. The approach is as much the experience as the destination — a 3-hour guided hike that moves through five distinct natural river pools, each one carved from the stone of a canyon that the river has been sculpting over millennia. The pools are cold, clear and deep enough to swim in, and the hike moves between submersion and walking in a rhythm that builds anticipation for the falls that wait at the end.

When the waterfall appears, it is impressive in the precise sense of that word: a cascade falling into a natural pool inside the canyon walls, surrounded entirely by tropical rainforest with no infrastructure, no crowd, nothing built. Guests swim beneath it. The time there is unhurried. The choice of descent — zip line over the canopy (Classic option) or tubing back down the river through rapids (Plus option) — both make sense from the experience already had. This tour is available from November to April only, when the river runs at the volume and force that makes all of this possible.

Seasonal Experience · November to April Only La Leona Waterfall runs at its full and magnificent capacity during the rainy and transition seasons. This experience is available November through April only. Outside of this window, water levels do not support safe access to the pools and canyon. Please plan your visit accordingly.

    The canyon hike at La Leona is unlike anything I had expected from a Costa Rica tour. The stone walls of the canyon rise on both sides as the trail descends into the river, and from that point forward you are moving through pools rather than walking on a path.
