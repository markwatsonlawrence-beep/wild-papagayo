🌊 Description

Experience an unforgettable waterfall adventure through one of Costa Rica’s most beautiful natural regions. Your journey begins with a relaxing 30-minute horseback ride to the stunning Chorrera Waterfall, featuring a refreshing sky-blue mineral pool ideal for swimming and nature lovers. Next, discover the iconic Oropendola Waterfall, an 82-foot (25-meter) natural wonder plunging into a sparkling turquoise pool hidden within a dramatic canyon setting. Complete the experience at the picturesque Río Negro Waterfall, where lush rainforest scenery and authentic outdoor adventure come together in one extraordinary day.
