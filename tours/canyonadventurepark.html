The Experience

Canyon Adventure Park is built for groups who want to do more than choose between a zip line and a raft — it combines four distinct adventures into a single private morning so the decision is already made. Eight zip line cables give a genuine bird's-eye perspective on the tropical forest canopy below, the kind of view that changes how you understand the scale of the environment you've been standing inside. The rappel brings you back to earth in a way that requires technique and nerve rather than speed. Rock climbing offers a different challenge entirely — deliberate, precise, and remarkably satisfying when the top is reached.

The rafting section closes the morning with 2 miles of Class II and III rapids through rainforest — enough white water to be genuinely exciting, with an experienced guide steering and calling instructions throughout. A local lunch.
