The Experience

Nature, Wellness & Volcanic Wonders

Discover the hidden beauty of Miravalles Volcano on a full-day experience that combines spectacular waterfalls, geothermal landscapes, volcanic wellness, and peaceful natural surroundings. Perfect for travelers seeking a relaxing adventure away from the crowds.

💦

Three Waterfalls

Visit three beautiful waterfalls surrounded by lush tropical forest, each offering its own unique scenery and incredible opportunities for photography and relaxation.

🌋

Volcanic Geothermal Area

Explore the fascinating geothermal activity of Miravalles Volcano, where steaming fumaroles, bubbling mud pools, and volcanic energy create an unforgettable natural spectacle.

🧖

Volcanic Mud Treatment

Cover your skin with mineral-rich volcanic mud before rinsing off and enjoying one of Costa Rica's most authentic natural spa experiences.

Natural Hot Springs
