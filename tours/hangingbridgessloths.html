The Experience

The hanging bridges experience is one of those activities that works for everyone because the vantage point it provides is genuinely unavailable from ground level. Moving across a suspension bridge through the tropical forest canopy — where the trees are at eye level rather than overhead, where the sounds of birds and insects come from all directions at once, and where the forest's three-dimensional complexity becomes visible in a way it never is from below — is an educational walk that never feels like one. Children marvel at being so close to the treetops. Adults find it equally disorienting and beautiful.

Three bridges are included in the circuit, each positioned at a different height within the canopy to reveal different layers of the ecosystem. The sloth encounter that follows is the culminating moment — a chance to observe these slow, peaceful creatures at close range in their natural forest setting. The guide ensures the encounter is intimate, respectful and genuinely informative. The day begins with Llanos de Cortez Waterfall, one of the most beautiful in all of Guanacaste, where guests can swim before the bridges..
