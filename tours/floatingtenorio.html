The Experience

The Tenorio River safari float is a different proposition entirely from the adventure-oriented experiences that dominate Guanacaste's tour offerings. There are no rapids to brace against, no adrenaline to manage. The river runs Class 1-2 — calm enough that your certified guide handles all the paddling and steering from the rear of the raft, leaving your group free to watch, photograph and absorb. Giant kapok trees and enormous strangler figs line both banks, their roots descending into the water. Wildlife reveals itself as the raft drifts past — birds, iguanas, monkeys in the canopy, and the occasional kingfisher moving downstream ahead of you.

The 90-minute float is preceded by a visit to Llanos de Cortez — one of the most beautiful waterfalls in all of Guanacaste, a broad curtain of white water falling into a clear swimming pool surrounded by forest. Together, the two elements make a half-day that is genuinely restorative. Children love it completely. The Plus option adds a stop at Las Pumas Wildlife Rescue Center, where injured and rehabilitated animals including jaguars, ocelots and tapirs are cared for and where a portion of every visit supports ongoing conservation work.
