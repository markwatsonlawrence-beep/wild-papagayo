The Experience

Llanos del Cortes is not a waterfall you simply look at. The river drops in a wide, powerful curtain into a pool of extraordinary clarity — and at its base, a crescent of white sand forms a beach that belongs nowhere near a jungle and yet is unmistakably here. You can watch from the shore or walk straight in. The mist, the roar, the cool of the water after the Guanacaste heat — this is one of those places that earns its reputation. Then the mood shifts entirely. Las Pumas Wildlife Rescue Center exists to give second chances to Costa Rica's most magnificent animals — pumas and jaguars that can no longer survive in the wild, ocelots recovering from injury, spider and white-faced monkeys learning to live again. Being close to these animals, guided by someone who understands them, is an experience that carries real weight. Lunch brings the day to a perfect close.
