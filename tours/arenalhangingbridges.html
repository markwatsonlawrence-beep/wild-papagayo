The Experience

Most people experience the Arenal rainforest from the ground. At Mistico Park, you experience it from inside the canopy — suspended on a series of hanging bridges that span between 5 and 100 metres above the forest floor, swaying gently as you walk, the full cathedral of tropical trees spreading beneath you in every direction. Toucans and tanagers move at eye level. Howler monkeys pass through the branches below. Orchids cling to the bridge cables. It is one of the most distinctive natural perspectives anywhere in Costa Rica.

Your certified bilingual guide walks every bridge with you, narrating the ecosystem in layers — what grows where and why, which birds are calling from which direction, what the forest sounds like when it is completely at rest. After the bridges, the day shifts dramatically downward: the 500-step staircase to the La Fortuna Waterfall, where 70 metres of water falls in a single unbroken column into a pool of extraordinary clarity. Guests may swim. For those choosing the ✦ PLUS option, the day closes at the thermal hot springs — geothermal pools fed by the volcanic system beneath Arenal, surrounded by jungle, the perfect counterpoint to a day spent entirely on your feet. Breakfast and lunch are included.

  • Difficulty: Medium. The hanging bridges involve walking on suspended structures at significant height — some guests may find this challenging if they are sensitive to heights or movement underfoot.
  • The La Fortuna Waterfall involves approximately 500 steps on a steep descent and equivalent ascent. Good mobility and a reasonable fitness level are required. Not suitable for guests with significant knee or joint limitations.
  • Guests are welcome to walk the bridges at their own pace. Your guide will adapt the walk to your group's comfort level.
  • The Arenal rainforest receives frequent rainfall. Light rain gear is strongly recommended regardless of season — rain on the bridges is an experience in itself, but dry layers for after are welcome.
  • Bring swimwear for the waterfall and (if choosing the Plus option) for the hot springs.
  • Wear sturdy closed-toe shoes or trail shoes. The waterfall staircase and bridge walkways require stable footing — sandals and flip-flops are not suitable.
  • Pack a light rain jacket or poncho. Brief showers are part of the Arenal rainforest experience.
  • Apply insect repellent before departure. The canopy environment is beautiful but active.
  • Bring a change of clothing, particularly if you plan to swim at the waterfall or use the hot springs.
  • A camera with a zoom lens or binoculars will enhance the bridge section significantly — the canopy perspective reveals birds and wildlife that are otherwise invisible from the trail below.
