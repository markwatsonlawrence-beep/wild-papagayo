The Experience

Congo Trail occupies a stretch of tropical forest where wildlife operates on its own schedule entirely independent of what is happening on the zip line cables below. Howler monkeys and white-faced monkeys forage for mangoes and bananas in the canopy while guests move through the forest on the zip line circuit — the animals are accustomed to the activity and carry on regardless, which means genuine wildlife encounters at eye level and above are simply part of the experience rather than something that requires a separate plan.

The main circuit runs 11 stations through the forest with three hanging bridges interspersed. The extreme section that closes the circuit offers three additional cables in a qualitatively different register: one involves the Superman position (horizontal, face down), one involves the Tarzan Swing. Plus continues after the zip line with two hours of ATV riding through the same forest and surrounding trails on the ground — a completely different perspective on the same landscape, and one that extends the morning's energy for guests who want more.
